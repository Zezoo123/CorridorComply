How to read this

A hit means a customer's name, alias form or identity number resembles a list entry closely enough to need a reviewer's decision; it is not a finding that the customer is a listed person. High is a near-exact name or an identity number with agreeing date of birth or nationality; medium and low are name resemblances the data could not exclude. A name match whose full date of birth differs from the listed person's is reported low, or medium when the name is near-exact. The reviewer's disposition, reason and time are the record kept for inspection under Law No. 20 of 2019 (ten years). The full row-by-row result, including clear rows, is in the CSV.
