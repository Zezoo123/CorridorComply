# Draft outsourcing clause (for the pilot agreement)

QCB AML/CFT Instructions item 6.7 apply when a financial institution outsources
any function to a third party: the institution and its Board remain responsible;
the service level agreement must bind the vendor to the AML/CFT Law and the
institution's own policies; and the institution's MLRO, QCB and the FIU must
have timely, unrestricted access to the vendor's records concerning the
institution's customers. This draft is a starting point for counsel, not legal
advice.

1. **Responsibility.** CorridorComply provides screening and decision-support
   software. All customer acceptance, sanctions, reporting and record-keeping
   decisions are made by the Institution and its MLRO. Nothing in the Service
   constitutes a decision of the Provider.
2. **Compliance.** The Provider shall, in performing the Service, comply with Law
   No. 20 of 2019, the QCB AML/CFT Instructions and the Institution's AML/CFT
   policies, procedures, systems and controls as notified to the Provider.
3. **Reporting.** Where the Provider becomes aware of a matter concerning the
   Institution's customers that may require a suspicious transaction report, it
   shall inform the Institution's MLRO without delay and shall not inform the
   customer.
4. **Access.** The Provider shall give the Institution's Board and MLRO, and on
   request the Qatar Central Bank and the Qatar Financial Information Unit,
   timely and unrestricted access to all records held by the Provider that relate
   to the Institution's customers or transactions. During the pilot the Service
   runs on the Institution's own systems and the Provider holds no such records.
5. **Data.** The Provider shall not receive, copy or retain the Institution's
   customer data except as expressly agreed in writing for a demonstration, and
   shall delete such data on completion.
6. **Foreign law.** If the law of another jurisdiction prevents any of the above,
   the Provider shall immediately inform the Institution so that the Institution
   may inform QCB.
7. **Lists and availability.** The Provider shall make sanctions list updates
   available within one business day of publication by the issuing authority and
   shall notify the Institution of any interruption exceeding [4] hours.
8. **Records.** Records generated by the Service are the Institution's and are
   retained by the Institution for at least ten years in line with Law No. 20 of
   2019.
